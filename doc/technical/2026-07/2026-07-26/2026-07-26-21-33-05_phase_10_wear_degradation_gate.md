## Technical Approach

1. Verify wear, contact, tolerance, and dataset-provenance evidence.
2. Audit reducer identity, session chronology, cumulative load cycles,
   lubrication, direct wear labels, and maintenance events.
3. Prevent temperature or operating-condition drift from becoming a false
   degradation state.
4. Classify `PINN-W1` through `PINN-W5` under the common taxonomy.
5. Preserve synthetic monotonic and Archard tests without authorizing
   real-data training.
