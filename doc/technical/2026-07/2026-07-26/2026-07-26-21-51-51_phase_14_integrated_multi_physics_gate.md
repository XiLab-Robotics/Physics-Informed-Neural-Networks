# Phase 14 Integrated Multi-Physics Gate

## Overview

Phase 14 evaluates whether the Wave 5.2 evidence supports an integrated
multi-physics PINN. Integration requires at least two complementary physical
components that independently passed their isolated curve-first,
identifiability, causality, and deployment gates. The document is
automatically approved under the user's active sixteen-phase authorization.

## Technical Approach

Use the Phase 13 roster and the isolated Phase 2 through Phase 12 decisions as
the evidence boundary. Audit the promoted-component count, shared variables,
units and scales, loss compatibility, interfaces, causal inputs,
identifiability, and deployment path. Classify every planned integration pair
without combining empirical, rejected, synthetic, offline, deferred, or
blocked mechanisms as if they were validated physics.
