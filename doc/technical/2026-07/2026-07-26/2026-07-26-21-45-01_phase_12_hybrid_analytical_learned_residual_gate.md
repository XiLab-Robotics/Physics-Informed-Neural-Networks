# Phase 12 Hybrid Analytical And Learned Residual Gate

## Overview

Phase 12 determines whether any analytical-plus-learned architecture inherited
from Phases 1 through 11 qualifies for a bounded full-PINN campaign. It
separates practical empirical hybrid models from full PINNs, which require a
validated differentiable physical consistency term in the training objective.
The document is automatically approved under the user's active sixteen-phase
authorization.

## Technical Approach

Audit the Phase 1 Polynomial-Fourier benchmark, the Phase 2 harmonic result,
the Phase 3 compliance result, and the hybrid-compensation reference synthesis.
Inventory the surviving analytical, empirical, target-only, causal, temporal,
directional, uncertainty, and deployment ingredients. Classify `PINN-R1`
through `PINN-R6`, while refusing full-PINN status when the analytical
component is only a semi-analytical predictor or when its physical constraint
was rejected by curve-first evidence.
